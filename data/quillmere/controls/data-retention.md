# Data retention and deletion

## retention — Retention while subscribed
Customer data is kept for as long as the customer's subscription is active, unless the
customer deletes it earlier.

## deletion — Deletion after termination
After a subscription ends, customer data is deleted from production systems within 30 days.
Copies in backups expire with the 35-day backup retention cycle.

## export — Data export
Workspace administrators can export all of their data in CSV or Parquet format at any time
during the subscription and for 30 days after it ends.

## residency — Data residency
Customers can choose to host their workspace in the EU region or the US region when the
workspace is created. The region cannot be changed after creation.
