# Incident response

## plan — Incident response plan
Quillmere maintains a written incident response plan that is reviewed once a year and after
every major incident. The plan defines severity levels, roles and communication steps.

## notification — Customer notification
Customers affected by a confirmed security incident involving their data are notified within
72 hours of confirmation. Notifications are sent to the security contact registered in the
customer's admin console.

## on-call — On-call coverage
An engineer is on call outside business hours for production outages. Security alerts raised
outside business hours are reviewed on the next business day unless they are classified as
critical.

## exercises — Exercises
A tabletop incident exercise is run twice a year with engineering and customer support.
