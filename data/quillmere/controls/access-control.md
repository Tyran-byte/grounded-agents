# Access control

## sso-mfa — Single sign-on and multi-factor authentication
Customers can enforce single sign-on through SAML 2.0 on the Business and Enterprise plans.
Multi-factor authentication is required for every Quillmere employee account without
exception.

## least-privilege — Least privilege for staff
Production access is granted per role and expires automatically after 8 hours. Engineers
request production access through a ticket that must be approved by a second engineer.
Quillmere support staff cannot read customer datasets unless the customer grants a
time-limited support session from its admin console.

## access-reviews — Access reviews
Access rights to production systems are reviewed every quarter by each system owner. Accounts
of departing employees are disabled within 24 hours of their last working day.

## audit-logs — Customer audit logs
Customer workspace audit logs record sign-ins, permission changes and data exports. Audit logs
are retained for 400 days and can be exported by workspace administrators.
