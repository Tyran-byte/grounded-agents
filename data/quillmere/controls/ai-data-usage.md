# AI features and data usage

## training — Model training
Customer data is not used to train or fine-tune machine learning models, whether those models
are built by Quillmere or by a third party.

## providers — Third-party model providers
The optional assistant feature sends the text of a user's question and the relevant chart
metadata to a third-party model provider listed as a subprocessor. Providers used by the
assistant are contractually barred from retaining prompts beyond 30 days.

## opt-out — Disabling AI features
Workspace administrators can disable the assistant feature for their whole workspace. When it
is disabled, no workspace data is sent to any model provider.

## human-review — Human review of AI output
Assistant answers are generated automatically and are not reviewed by Quillmere staff before
they are shown to the user.
